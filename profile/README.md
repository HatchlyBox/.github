<h2 align="center">Hatchlybox</h2>

<p align="center"><b>Say it. Hatch it.</b></p>

<p align="center">
  Describe an app or a game, and AI builds it, installs it and keeps it in one library across all your devices.
</p>

---

Type what you want, such as *"a budget tracker with a pie chart"* or *"a neon racing game"*, and an AI helper
(Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode, under your own account) writes it, builds it and installs it on the device you're
using. On Android it can also try out what it made, with you watching every tap. Then ask for the same creation on
another system, and keep everything in one library synced between your phones and computers.

No Hatchlybox account, no analytics, no server of ours in between. Free software under the GNU AGPL 3.0.

### Repositories

- [**hatchlybox-android**](https://github.com/HatchlyBox/hatchlybox-android): the Android app, with its home screen, widgets and phone actions.
- [**hatchlybox**](https://github.com/HatchlyBox/hatchlybox): the engine that makes, builds and syncs creations, and Hatchlybox for computers.
